# Points of Interest: Encounter Pitches (Zones 13–19)

Chapter 8 onward uses **Zones 13–19**, and each zone's level matches its encounter level. On each table a d20 roll of 1–5 sends you to the previous zone's table, so those rows are left out. Several creatures appear in more than one zone; each gets one pitch, with the stronger versions in later zones listed alongside it.

> Each pitch below gives a reason the King would have marked the spot.

## Zone 13: Tourney Fields (Level 13)



### 1. The Shadowless Lodge

**Encounter:** Ankou (1 in Z13, 2 in Z14–15, 3 in Z16, 4 in Z17, 6 elite in Z18)

The King marked a ruined hunting lodge with "scouts lost — 3rd time." A scout's body lies on the threshold, and the corpse casts no shadow. An ankou, one of the Fey Queen's silent assassins, waits in the rafters to test the PCs. This site could become a recurring thread: each time the PCs visit a later site the Fey Queen has touched, more ankous are waiting, and the book's table already scales up the same way. It gives an early, concrete taste of the Fey Queen before Chapter 10.

### 2. The Perfect Song

**Encounter:** 3 cauthoojes, Moderate 13

The Royal Academy sent a troupe of composers to transcribe a melody that herders hear at dusk in a meadow in the Tourney Fields. The composers are still there. They sit hypnotized among half-written scores, starving, while cauthoojes warble from the tall grass. The PCs must resist the song, kill the birds and save the musicians. The scores could become a kingdom art treasure.

## Zone 14: Lowlands (Level 14)

### 3. Nine Mouths, One Belly

**Encounter:** Jotund troll, Low 14 (Severe 12 in Zone 12)

A nine-headed troll has made its den in a moor cairn. The King's note says only "bargained with it — no." His envoy's head sits on a stake outside, and one of the troll's heads talks in the envoy's voice. The heads argue with each other, so the PCs can turn them against each other with Diplomacy or Deception before or during the fight.

### 4. The Gardeners

**Encounter:** 4 athaches, Moderate 14 (6 elite athaches, Moderate 16, in Zone 16)

A family of three-armed athaches has turned a ruined orchard estate into a grotesque garden with "statues" of petrified, venom-paralyzed travellers. The King marked the estate as a former royal noble holding he wanted to reclaim. A few victims are still alive and can be rescued.

## Zone 16: Uplands (Level 16)

### 5. The Ossuary Wall

**Encounter:** 2 skulltakers, Moderate 16 (Low 19 in Zone 19)

A hill-fort ruin built from stacked skulls, older than the King's city. The King marked it as a "curiosity for the Academy" and sent a team of antiquarians who never came back. Two skulltakers, undead masses of fused bone, now patrol the walls, and the antiquarians' skulls are the newest stones. This pairs well with ruins tied to the hill-clan or giant history of the uplands.

### 6. The Larder

**Encounter:** 4 hill giant butchers, Moderate 16 (Moderate 17 in Zone 17)

The Giant Lord's butchers run a smokehouse camp in a hollow. They catch travellers here and especially like adventurers, because adventurers carry treasure. The King marked it as "The Giant Lord's men — tolerate," which hints at a quiet arrangement between the King and the giants. Captives in cages can confirm that arrangement, and a wanted poster of one of the PCs might hang on the wall.

### 7. The Wyvern Queen's Hunting Ground

**Encounter:** The Wyvern Queen, Severe 16 (also Zone 17)

A fallen ridge strewn with bones and a dead mammoth hung in a tree. The King marked it with a crude sketch of wings and "do NOT." The legendary wyvern buzzes the PCs to scare them and only fights if attacked. She flees at 100 HP, and if the PCs let her go she returns later looking for revenge. That makes this a natural two-part encounter, and her lair at BR3 is already a keyed site.

## Zone 17: Skyfall Wastes (Level 17)

The book says these skyfall constructs can be ancient robots with weakness 15 to critical hits and electricity. The three construct pitches below assume you use that version.

### 8. The Lightwardens

**Encounter:** 2 radiant skyfall wardens, Moderate 17

A wedge of silvery hull juts from the hillside, still guarded by two floating constructs that fire graviton beams. The King traded with skyfall scavengers and marked this site as "unopened — buyers in the salvage towns." The constructs guard a sealed hatch. Behind it you could place a skymetal cache or tech item for the treasure budget.

### 9. The Iron Juggernaut

**Encounter:** Elite adamantine golem, Moderate 17

A tracked war machine rolls the same patrol loop it has followed for ten thousand years, grinding a trench into the hills. The King's note reads "adamantine — HOW MUCH?" He wanted the metal. The PCs can kill it for salvage, or a high Crafting or Thievery check could take control of it.

### 10. The Scout's Wager

**Encounter:** Wild Hunt scout + 2 hounds, Moderate 17

A ram-horned scout has claimed a crashed skyfall pod as a trophy blind. The King marked the spot after one of his own agents wronged the Hunt. The scout now wants revenge on anyone carrying royal goods, and the PCs probably have some after looting the palace. This makes a good one-off duel that sets up the Hunt's grudges.

### 11. The Unmaker

**Encounter:** Elite guthallath, Severe 17

A colossal construct stands half-buried and dormant at the centre of a glassed crater. A haze of deconstructor nanites eats anything that comes close. The King marked it "the Colossus — wake it for the war?" and his engineers left half-finished control sigils on its chassis. The PCs might arrive just as the King's leftover agents finally wake it.

## Zone 18: Whispering Wood (Level 18)

### 12. The Storm Tree

**Encounter:** Sard, Low 18

At the forest's edge a vast tree scuttles on spidery roots with lightning running under its blackened bark. One of the elder fey woke it. The King's woodcutters marked it as "the tree that moves" after losing an entire logging camp. The scorched clearing and the camp's wreckage make a good first step into the Whispering Wood.

### 13. The Quilled Hunter

**Encounter:** Primal bandersnatch, Low 18 (2 at Moderate 19 in Zone 19)

A six-legged quilled cat the size of a house hunts only clever prey. The King marked a canyon where the bandersnatch keeps trophies of the cleverest creatures it has killed, among them a royal ranger's bow. The bandersnatch stalks the PCs for hours before striking, which makes a strong exploration-tension set piece.

### 14. The Shadow Court

**Encounter:** 6 elite ankous, Moderate 18

This is the payoff for #1. A grove of dead trees hung with ankou cloaks serves as their staging ground under the Fey Queen. The King's last note here reads "they watched me. they let me leave." If the PCs left an ankou alive earlier, it might be here as a fixer who reveals something about the Fey Queen.

### 15. The Moody Brood

**Encounter:** 3 whimwyrms, Moderate 18 (4 at Moderate 19 in Zone 19)

A grove where seasons change every few minutes and whimwyrms play. The King marked it "charming!! bring the court." His courtiers found out how fast a whimwyrm's mood turns. Play it as a social encounter that can flip to violence, with the wyrms' mood moving up and down a tracker based on how the PCs act.

### 16. The Trickster's Emissary

**Encounter:** Vilderavn herald on a zomok, Moderate 18. Only before Chapter 10; it becomes a Wild Hunt encounter after that.

A raven-fey on a plant dragon lands in front of the PCs, claims to serve the Fey Queen and orders them back to tend their realm. In truth the herald wants the PCs' kingdom to flourish. They fight only to drive the PCs off, never to kill. The King marked "the raven" because it once landed in front of him and gave him the same speech, which unnerved him. This is the best foreshadowing piece for Chapter 11 on the whole table.

### 17. Spore-Dawn

**Encounter:** Mu spore, Severe 18

Mu spores are said to appear over cities before apocalyptic events. The King's astrologers tracked one drifting over the forest's heart and wrote "omen — of what?" The PCs find it anchored over a hollow, seeding the trees. Whether it's a herald of the Fey Queen's coming move or an independent horror is your call. A 100-foot flying fungus makes a memorable boss fight.

## Zone 19: Western Mountains (Level 19)

### 18. The Linnorm's Pass

**Encounter:** Tor linnorm, Moderate 19

A mountain pass the King wanted as a western trade route, marked "blocked — serpent." The tor linnorm lairs in an avalanche chute. Because linnorms curse their killers, the PCs face a real cost-benefit choice: kill it to open the pass, or find another way to move it.

### 19. Shadow on the Peaks

**Encounter:** The black dragon, Moderate 19

Before Chapter 10 this is a sighting: the black dragon circles her old haunts and the PCs can follow her to the abandoned mortal lair. The King marked it "her old roost — empty?" After the PCs fight her in her den in the fey realm, the encounter turns into a revenge ambush, and this site is where she finds them.

### 20. The Burning Giant

**Encounter:** Elite lerritan, Severe 19

An obsidian-skinned fire giant has been burning its way down the valleys. The King marked a line of scorched hexes heading toward the King's city and wrote "toward us — or toward them?" If you want a high-stakes kingdom threat, the burn line could point at one of the PCs' own settlements, which forces the party to intercept it.
