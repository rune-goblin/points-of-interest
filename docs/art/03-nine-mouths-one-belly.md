# 03. Nine Mouths, One Belly — Art

## Map note
A crude nine-pointed star with a stick figure inside it. Beside the star, a separate tiny head drawn on top of a pole. Beneath: "bargained with it — no."

## Tactical map
A grey-green peat moor dotted with cairns and dark bog pools. A long barrow mound rises in the centre, its entrance framed by standing stones carved with spirals. Beside the entrance, a wooden stake bearing a severed head, with a leather satchel hung below it. Inside the barrow: a long earthen chamber, floor carpeted with bones and dented armour, an alcove at the far end holding a cairn of huge, ancient bones draped in faded hides. Outside, patches of soft bog mark the approaches.

## Characters

### The Jotund Troll
- **Portrait:** A towering, hunched troll with mossy green-grey hide and nine heads on long, sinewy necks sprouting from its shoulders. The heads all argue at once. One head wears a neatly oiled moustache and a courtier's gold earring, mimicking a gentleman. Three hang drooling and hungry; three snarl with scarred brows; two are grey, wrinkled and half-asleep.
- **Token:** Seen from above, nine heads fanned out in different directions like a crown of serpents, claws planted on bones.

### The Envoy (troll head)
- **Portrait:** Close on one troll head with a waxed moustache, a gold earring and a raised, supercilious brow, speaking with the poise of a diplomat.
- **Token:** Not needed separately; used as a speaking portrait only.

### The Old Heads (troll heads)
- **Portrait:** Two grey, sagging heads with milky eyes and drooping lips, tilted toward the bone cairn in the alcove.
- **Token:** Not needed separately; used as a speaking portrait only.

### Ser Halward Toll (dead envoy)
- **Portrait:** In life: a polished royal courtier with an oiled moustache, a green-and-gold sash and a confident diplomat's smile. In death: the same face on a stake, with a crow perched on the pole.
- **Token:** A head on a stake with a satchel hanging beneath.
