# 08. The Lightwardens — Art

## Map note
A small silver-ink wedge shape with three concentric rings drawn around it. Beside it: "unopened — buyers in Starfall."

## Tactical map
A grassy hillside with a huge wedge of silvery hull buried at a steep angle, its seams glowing with pale light and a round sealed hatch on its exposed flank. In front of the hull, a wide ring of glassy, scorched earth. Three tall metal pylons with lens heads stand in an arc inside the ring. Within the ring lie skeletons in Pitaxian livery, a dropped crowbar, a broken lantern and a pack spilling tools. At the edge of the ring, a large brass-and-glass device of nested rings and lenses stands motionless. Long grass and scattered boulders surround the scorched area.

## Characters

### Radiant warden (dormant)
- **Portrait:** A large construct disguised as an orrery: nested brass rings, glass lenses and a central sphere of pale light, mounted on a squat tripod base. Faint lines of light trace the seams.
- **Token:** Rings at rest around the glowing core.

### Radiant warden (active)
- **Portrait:** The same construct unfolded into a towering guardian: brass rings spinning around a core of blazing light, one arm ending in a massive hammer of polished metal, the other a lens focusing a beam.
- **Token:** Rings spinning, hammer arm raised, beam lens glowing.

### Defence pylon
- **Portrait:** A tall, slim metal pylon topped by a rotating cluster of lenses, scorched at the base, humming with light.
- **Token:** A ring of lenses seen from above, one lens glowing red.
