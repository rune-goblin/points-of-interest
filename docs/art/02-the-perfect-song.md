# 02. The Perfect Song — Art

## Map note
A single bar of music staff with five notes, the last note circled three times hard enough to tear the paper. Beside it: "THE PERFECT SONG — Academy to transcribe. Report by Rova."

## Tactical map
A high meadow at sunset, waist-high golden grass rolling toward a line of dark woods. At the centre, a trampled circle where four figures sit among drifts of paper weighted by stones, inkpots and a violin case. A ring of flattened grass about sixty feet out marks where the birds pace. At its edge, goat skulls and scattered bones, and one human hand still in a velvet cuff. A herders' track runs down one side toward the tourney fields below; a lone shepherd's hut stands at the woodline.

## Characters

### Maestra Ilsabet Rova (composer, leader)
- **Portrait:** A woman in her fifties with silver hair escaping a once-elegant braid, gaunt and hollow-cheeked, wearing a stained velvet Academy coat with gold piping. Ink stains her fingers to the knuckle. Her eyes are bright, wet and fixed on something far away; her lips shape a tune.
- **Token:** Cross-legged in grass, bent over a sheaf of pages, quill raised mid-stroke.

### Pell (youngest composer)
- **Portrait:** A boy of about seventeen, skeletal, collarbones sharp under a too-large shirt, a cracked lute in his lap. He smiles beatifically, unaware he is dying.
- **Token:** Slumped on his side in the grass, still clutching the lute.

### Daro Vesk (composer, percussionist)
- **Portrait:** A broad man gone loose and sallow, beard grown wild, tapping rhythms on his knees with bloodied fingertips. A drum lies split beside him.
- **Token:** Sitting upright, hands raised mid-beat.

### Olenna Fair (composer, copyist)
- **Portrait:** A neat, bespectacled woman whose court dress has been carefully mended, writing music in tiny precise notation across blank staves that show nothing at all.
- **Token:** Kneeling, writing on a page held flat against a stone.

### Cauthooj (first, the lead singer)
- **Portrait:** A tall flightless bird with a long neck and a heavy hooked beak, iridescent blue-black plumage, its throat puffed out and trembling with song. Its eyes are bright gold and unblinking.
- **Token:** Head thrown back, throat swollen in song.

### Cauthooj (second)
- **Portrait:** Leaner and greyer, with a torn crest and a scar across its beak, crouched low in the grass with its head swaying side to side.
- **Token:** Stalking low through grass, neck stretched forward.

### Cauthooj (third)
- **Portrait:** The youngest, smaller, with patchy juvenile feathers and goat's blood on its beak, standing on one leg in a pose of strange, alert grace.
- **Token:** Mid-hop, one leg tucked, wings flared for balance.
