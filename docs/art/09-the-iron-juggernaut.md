# 09. The Iron Juggernaut — Art

## Map note
A dotted loop drawn around a stretch of empty plain with an arrow showing the direction of travel. Written beside it twice: "adamantine — HOW MUCH?" The second time is underlined. Below, in a shakier hand: "salvagers not back."

## Tactical map
Flat grassland crossed by a deep trench worn smooth by ten thousand years of tracks, its walls crumbling. At the northern bend, a rusted gantry of girders straddles the trench, scorched where something docks. At the southern bend, the trench ends in a glassed crater with a few stumps of ancient foundations. Beside the trench, an abandoned salvage camp: a toppled wagon, pry bars and chains, a collapsed tent, and a cracked plate of dark grey metal propped against the wagon. A few lone boulders in the grass.

## Interior map (cargo hold)
A long, low metal hold lit through cracks in the hull. The floor is deep in bones and rags. Scratched tally marks cover every wall, old ones worn faint, the newest sharp and bright. A dorsal hatch overhead. Two living captives huddle at the far end beside an empty water skin.

## Characters

### The Iron Juggernaut
- **Portrait:** A house-sized hauler on two wide tracks, plated in dark grey adamantine scarred by ages of travel. Steam vents and laser emitters line its flanks. Two jointed manipulator arms fold against its front. A dorsal hatch sits on its back, and faded Numerian delivery glyphs cover its prow.
- **Token:** Seen from above, a long armoured hull on twin tracks, arms folded forward, hatch on top.

### Brann Kesk (starving salvager)
- **Portrait:** A broad-shouldered human man in a torn leather work apron, gaunt and hollow-cheeked, goggles pushed up on his forehead, a crowbar in his weakening grip.
- **Token:** Slumped against a wall, crowbar across his knees.

### Ottilie Kesk (starving salvager)
- **Portrait:** A wiry human woman with cropped hair and soot on her cheeks, cracked lips, a tool belt hanging loose on her thin frame, scratching a fresh tally with a nail.
- **Token:** Kneeling, one hand raised to the wall mid-scratch.

### Tarku (remains)
- **Portrait:** A Kellid skeleton in rotted furs and bone ornaments, braids still bound with beads, a stone-tipped spear wrapped in a charm across its lap. The longest tally on the wall runs above it.
- **Token:** A seated skeleton with a spear across its knees.
