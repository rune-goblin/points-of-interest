# 07. The Wyvern Queen's Hunting Ground — Art

## Map note
A crude winged shape with a long barbed tail, scratched so hard the pen tore the vellum. Beneath it, in shaky capitals: "do NOT."

## Tactical map
A collapsed ridge seen from above: a slope of grey shale running down from south to north, crossed by snapped and fallen pines. Near the centre stands one tall dead pine with a mammoth carcass wedged in its crown. The north edge breaks off into a deep gorge, shown as a sharp drop with mist below. Bones of horses and men lie scattered in the shale, and a round shield with a crest of a laurel and mask lies half-buried near the cliff edge. A game trail enters from the south through standing pines.

## Characters

### Minognos-Ushad (legendary wyvern)
- **Portrait:** A huge wyvern with scarred, slate-green scales and a ragged left wing membrane patched with old scar tissue. Her barbed stinger drips amber venom. One eye is milky from an old arrow wound; the other burns yellow. A broken hunting spear juts from her shoulder, long healed over.
- **Token:** Wings spread wide from above, tail curled forward over her back with the stinger poised, the old spear visible in her shoulder.

### Minognos-Ushad (diving)
- **Portrait:** The same wyvern in a steep dive, talons forward and wings folded back, jaws open.
- **Token:** Wings swept back in a dive, talons extended forward.
