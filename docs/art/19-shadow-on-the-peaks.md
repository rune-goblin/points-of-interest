# 19. Shadow on the Peaks — Art

## Map note
A black spire shaded in heavy ink. Above it, a wing shape drawn again and again, the lines growing darker and heavier with each pass. The note reads: "her old roost — empty? if so, mine."

## Tactical map
A black-rock spire rising out of a misty fen of dark, still water. The fen is dotted with dead trees and pools stained yellow-green. A narrow, broken path winds up the spire to a ledge near its top, where an open cave mouth is strewn with bones and pitted metal. Old wooden survey stakes with faded green ribbons ring the cave entrance.

## Characters

### Ilthuliak (black dragon)
- **Portrait:** A huge black dragon with horns that sweep forward, acid dripping from her jaws and hissing where it lands. A long, pale scar runs along her flank. Her eyes are bright green and narrowed with hatred.
- **Token (sighting):** Seen from above: the dragon in flight, wings spread wide, her shadow falling on fen water.
- **Token (ambush):** Seen from above: the dragon rising from black water, head lowered and jaws open, water streaming from her wings.
