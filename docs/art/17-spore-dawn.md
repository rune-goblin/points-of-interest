# 17. Spore-Dawn — Art

## Map note
A circle filled with tiny ink dots, an arrow pointing down into its centre, and the words "omen — of what?" in a careful astrologer's script. Below, in a heavier, angrier hand: "burn the astrologer."

## Tactical map
A sinkhole in a grey, dying forest. The trees around the rim have peeling bark and pale spore-dust drifting from their trunks; twenty of them bear visible fungal growths. The sinkhole drops to a floor carpeted in white mould and fungal stalks. On one edge of the rim, a collapsed wooden hunting blind lies half-buried in mould, a body slumped inside. Above the sinkhole hangs the vast bulk of the mu spore, its tentacles trailing down into the trees below.

## Characters

### The Mu Spore
- **Portrait:** A colossal flying mass of fungus and flesh, ridged and pale grey, with dozens of long tentacles trailing beneath. A huge maw opens in its underside, ringed with teeth. Clouds of spores vent from gill-like slits along its sides. Below it, tiny trees give a sense of its size.
- **Token:** Seen from above: a vast round shape with ridged, mottled skin and tentacles radiating outward in every direction, a dark ring at its centre where the maw lies.

### The Court Astrologer (deceased)
- **Portrait:** A thin older man in faded star-patterned robes, a brass astrolabe hanging from his neck, ink-stained fingers clutching a notebook. His face is gaunt and worried.
- **Token:** Seen from above: a mould-covered body slumped in the wreckage of the blind, notebook still in hand.
