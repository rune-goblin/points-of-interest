# 10. The Scout's Wager — Art

## Map note
A curled ram's horn drawn in green ink beside a small crater. Beneath it: "Varga — never again. Pay them nothing."

## Tactical map
A shallow crater with sloping sides. At its bottom lies a blackened, split-open metal escape pod. Green threads run from the pod's ribs to stakes around the crater floor, strung with antlers, skulls and a torn Pitaxian cloak. Patches of fused glass shine across the crater floor. Low fog pools in the deepest parts. A skeleton in leather armour lies inside the pod beside a satchel. Grassland and scattered rocks surround the rim.

## Characters

### Sileth (Wild Hunt scout)
- **Portrait:** A compact fey with curling ram's horns, glowing green eyes and a sharp, amused smile. Sileth wears leaf-green leathers and holds a scimitar of crystallised green force. A shard of the same crystal glows in the other hand. Green threads are knotted around both wrists.
- **Token:** Perched in a crouch, scimitar held low, horns prominent.

### Ash-Tongue (Wild Hunt hound)
- **Portrait:** A lean, smoke-grey hound with glowing green eyes and a mane of green mist, jaws open in a bay. Faint ghost-faces swirl in the mist around it.
- **Token:** Mid-lunge, jaws open, mist trailing.

### Thornfoot (Wild Hunt hound)
- **Portrait:** A heavier, darker hound with scars across its muzzle and thorns tangled in its coat, green mist rising from its back. Its head is low, stalking.
- **Token:** Stalking low, head down, eyes glowing.

### Varga Tess (dead agent)
- **Portrait:** A skeleton in worn leather armour with a Pitaxian dagger on its belt, slumped inside the pod, a stag antler tine in one bony hand.
- **Token:** Slumped skeleton clutching an antler tine.
