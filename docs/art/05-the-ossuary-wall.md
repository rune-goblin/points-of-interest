# 05. The Ossuary Wall — Art

## Map note
A ring of small circles drawn like stacked skulls, labelled "curiosity for the Academy — 4 antiquarians sent." Later, in darker ink, a single heavy X through the ring.

## Tactical map
A hilltop ruin on a windswept ridge. An outer ring of tumbled grey stone surrounds an inner wall made entirely of skulls, packed tight and weathered smooth, with three breaches in it. Near the top of the wall, four fresh white skulls, one still wearing wire spectacles. The inner court is open ground strewn with bone fragments, with a fallen standing stone at its centre hiding a sealed burial chamber beneath. Outside the wall, an abandoned scholars' camp: tents, a collapsed easel, sketchbooks blowing open in the wind, a surveyor's chain stretched across the grass.

## Characters

### The Skulltaker
- **Portrait:** A huge serpentine undead built from thousands of fused skulls and spines, its body coiling like a bone dragon, a storm of bone shards swirling around it. Its head is a cluster of interlocking skulls with a glow deep in the eye sockets. Four white skulls set in its brow still wear spectacles and a scholar's cap.
- **Token:** Coiled in a ring above the court, head raised, a halo of bone shards around it.

### Magister Corwen Ash (dead antiquarian, voice in the wall)
- **Portrait:** In life: a fussy, balding scholar with wire spectacles and a quill behind his ear, Academy robes splashed with ink. In death: his spectacled skull set in the top course of the wall.
- **Token:** Not needed; voice portrait only.

### Dalia Sorn (dead antiquarian)
- **Portrait:** In life: a young field sketcher with a smudged charcoal pencil and a wide-brimmed hat. In death: a small white skull with charcoal smudges still on the brow.
- **Token:** Not needed; voice portrait only.

### Hemmet Brask (dead antiquarian)
- **Portrait:** In life: a burly surveyor in mud-caked boots carrying the surveyor's chain. In death: a broad skull with a gold tooth.
- **Token:** Not needed; voice portrait only.

### Lio Venn (dead antiquarian)
- **Portrait:** In life: an elderly linguist with a long white beard and a satchel of rubbings. In death: a skull with a scrap of beard still caught in its jaw.
- **Token:** Not needed; voice portrait only.

### Skeletal Champion (risen victim, optional)
- **Portrait:** A skeleton in mixed hill-clan and Academy remnants, bone shards orbiting its skull, wielding a rusted hill-clan axe.
- **Token:** Striding forward, axe raised, shards trailing behind.
