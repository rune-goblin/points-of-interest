# 15. The Moody Brood — Art

## Map note
A circle divided into four wedges like a sundial, each coloured for a season: green, gold, rust and white. Two tiny curled dragons perch on its rim. Underneath: "charming!! bring the court." Below that, struck through twice: "never again."

## Tactical map
A round grove divided into four quarters by season. Spring has blossoming trees and muddy ground. Summer has lush grass. Autumn has falling red leaves. Winter has snow-covered ground and frosted branches. A large mossy stone sits at the centre where the quarters meet. Beside it lies a ruined banquet table with overturned goblets, a jester's cap and a broken lute. Silver plates lie scattered in the grass.

## Characters

### Pippet (whimwyrm)
- **Portrait:** A slender, sleek dragon no longer than a person, scaled in dawn colours of rose, gold and pale pink. Large, bright eyes and a grin full of tiny needle teeth. A frill of feathery spines runs down its neck.
- **Token:** Coiled on the mossy stone, head up, tail flicking, mouth open mid-laugh.

### Gloamsy (whimwyrm)
- **Portrait:** A slender dragon of the same size, scaled in dusk colours of violet, indigo and smoky grey. Half-lidded eyes and a sly smile. A thin wisp of magenta mist curls from her nostrils.
- **Token:** Lying stretched along the stone's edge, chin on her claws, eyeing the viewer sideways.
