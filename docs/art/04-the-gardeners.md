# 04. The Gardeners — Art

## Map note
The outline of a manor with formal hedges drawn as neat rectangles, and three small upright stick figures standing between them. Beneath: "Varrold estate — reclaim after the war. Statues??"

## Tactical map
The roofless shell of a stone manor at one end, its upper floor collapsed into rubble. In front of it, a formal garden of overgrown topiary hedges forming a maze of corridors, with stone plinths at the junctions. Five plinths hold living people propped upright and bound with vines. A stinking compost pit of mulch and bones sits in a corner of the garden, and a broken fountain stands at the maze's heart. Shears, rakes and a huge wheelbarrow lie by the manor steps.

## Characters

### Matriarch Gorm (athach, family head)
- **Portrait:** A massive three-armed giantess with grey, warty skin, tusk-like fangs propping her mouth open, drool glistening down her chin. She wears a filthy gardener's apron over rags and a crown of dead roses. Her third arm holds huge garden shears.
- **Token:** Standing on manor rubble, a boulder raised overhead in two arms, shears in the third.

### Rorrik (athach, Gorm's mate)
- **Portrait:** Bigger and dumber than Gorm, one tusk snapped off, a stolen noble's cloak tied around his neck like a bib. His third arm is shrivelled and twitchy.
- **Token:** Hunched beside a wall, hurling masonry.

### Thud (adolescent athach)
- **Portrait:** A gangly young athach with a lopsided grin, too-long arms and a trophy necklace of belt buckles.
- **Token:** Crouched at a hedge corner, ready to pounce.

### Snip (adolescent athach)
- **Portrait:** A wiry young athach who copies its mother: a tiny apron, a stolen pair of hand pruners in the third claw, carefully trimming a hedge into the shape of a person.
- **Token:** Clipping a hedge, half-turned toward an intruder.

### Mulch (adolescent athach)
- **Portrait:** Fat and lazy, smeared in compost, chewing on a goat leg.
- **Token:** Sitting at the edge of the compost pit, about to rise.

### Bramble (adolescent athach)
- **Portrait:** The smallest and cruellest, with briars woven into its matted hair and venom dripping from its third claw, grinning at a statue-victim.
- **Token:** Leaning over a plinth, claw poised above a victim's neck.

### Captain Mira Vell (living statue, royal officer)
- **Portrait:** A hard-faced woman in a torn green royal officer's coat, hair cropped short, wrists bound by vines, her face slack from venom except for furious, tracking eyes.
- **Token:** Upright on a plinth, vines around her body, eyes turned toward the viewer.

### Haddo (living statue, farmer)
- **Portrait:** A burly farmer with sunburned arms and a straw hat, limp and grey-lipped.
- **Token:** Propped on a plinth, chin on his chest.

### Ysolde (living statue, pedlar)
- **Portrait:** A thin travelling pedlar with ribbons and trinkets still hanging from her coat, lips cracked, mouth open.
- **Token:** Propped on a plinth, arms tied out like a scarecrow.

### Brother Amat (living statue, wandering priest)
- **Portrait:** An elderly priest of the hunter god in a muddy brown robe, holy symbol still around his neck, eyes closed in prayer.
- **Token:** Kneeling on a plinth, bound in place.

### Tobin (living statue, boy)
- **Portrait:** A boy of about ten in a herder's smock, eyes wide and terrified, the only one able to move his fingers.
- **Token:** Small figure on a plinth, curled tight.
