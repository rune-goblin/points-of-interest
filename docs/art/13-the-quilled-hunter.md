# 13. The Quilled Hunter — Art

## Map note
A long, crooked line for the canyon, with a small cat drawn at its end: six legs and a back of quills like a hedgehog. Above it: "Wren's bow is here. Wren isn't." A row of small crosses runs along both sides of the canyon line.

## Tactical map
The den at the canyon's end. Steep rock walls on both sides close overhead under a forest canopy, letting in green light. Trophies hang from the walls on vine nets: a manticore's tail, a cracked iron crown, a small dragon skull. Two dense thorn thickets fill the middle of the canyon floor. A fallen log bridges a narrow crevasse near the entrance. At the far end, a sandy hollow lies under a huge fallen tree, scattered with bones, a ranger's pack and an unstrung longbow.

## Characters

### Primal bandersnatch
- **Portrait:** An enormous tawny great cat with six legs and no ears. Barbed quills run along its back and down a long, flexible tail. Its eyes are wide and hypnotic, with spiralling pupils.
- **Token:** Seen from above: crouched to spring, tail curled, quills raised along its spine.

### Wren Ashby (remains)
- **Portrait:** A lean ranger with cropped grey hair and a weathered face, wearing a Pitaxian green cloak and a quiver at the hip. This is a sketch from the front of Wren's journal.
- **Token:** Not used in play. A green cloak lies torn in the den, pinned beneath a quill.
