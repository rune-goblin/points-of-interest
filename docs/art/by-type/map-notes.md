# Map Notes

The King's scrawls on the campaign map, one per site.

## 01. The Shadowless Lodge

A quick ink sketch of a lodge with an antlered gable. Beside it, where a shadow should fall, an empty circle traced twice. Scrawled beneath: "scouts lost — 3rd time. No shadows. Who?"

Source: [01-the-shadowless-lodge.md](../01-the-shadowless-lodge.md)

## 02. The Perfect Song

A single bar of music staff with five notes, the last note circled three times hard enough to tear the paper. Beside it: "THE PERFECT SONG — Academy to transcribe. Report by Rova."

Source: [02-the-perfect-song.md](../02-the-perfect-song.md)

## 03. Nine Mouths, One Belly

A crude nine-pointed star with a stick figure inside it. Beside the star, a separate tiny head drawn on top of a pole. Beneath: "bargained with it — no."

Source: [03-nine-mouths-one-belly.md](../03-nine-mouths-one-belly.md)

## 04. The Gardeners

The outline of a manor with formal hedges drawn as neat rectangles, and three small upright stick figures standing between them. Beneath: "Varrold estate — reclaim after the war. Statues??"

Source: [04-the-gardeners.md](../04-the-gardeners.md)

## 05. The Ossuary Wall

A ring of small circles drawn like stacked skulls, labelled "curiosity for the Academy — 4 antiquarians sent." Later, in darker ink, a single heavy X through the ring.

Source: [05-the-ossuary-wall.md](../05-the-ossuary-wall.md)

## 06. The Larder

A butcher's cleaver sketched in quick strokes, driven into the ground beside a hollow in the uplands. Beneath it: "Brutal, but ours. Tolerate." To the side, a tally of five marks with a single line struck through them.

Source: [06-the-larder.md](../06-the-larder.md)

## 07. The Wyvern Queen's Hunting Ground

A crude winged shape with a long barbed tail, scratched so hard the pen tore the vellum. Beneath it, in shaky capitals: "do NOT."

Source: [07-the-wyvern-queens-hunting-ground.md](../07-the-wyvern-queens-hunting-ground.md)

## 08. The Lightwardens

A small silver-ink wedge shape with three concentric rings drawn around it. Beside it: "Sealed skyfall."

Source: [08-the-lightwardens.md](../08-the-lightwardens.md)

## 09. The Iron Juggernaut

A dotted loop drawn around a stretch of empty plain with an arrow showing the direction of travel. Written beside it twice: "adamantine — HOW MUCH?" The second time is underlined. Below, in a shakier hand: "salvagers not back."

Source: [09-the-iron-juggernaut.md](../09-the-iron-juggernaut.md)

## 10. The Scout's Wager

A curled ram's horn drawn in green ink beside a small crater. Beneath it: "Varga — never again. Pay them nothing."

Source: [10-the-scouts-wager.md](../10-the-scouts-wager.md)

## 11. The Unmaker

A small circle hatched solid black, with a towering stick figure inside it, arms raised. Beside it in the King's looping hand: "the Colossus — wake it for the war?" Below, in a steadier hand: "Vane says three days. Pay him double." A thumbprint of soot smudges one corner.

Source: [11-the-unmaker.md](../11-the-unmaker.md)

## 12. The Storm Tree

A tree sketched with spider legs in place of roots and a jagged lightning bolt through its trunk. Underneath: "the tree that moves." Beside it, eleven tally marks and the words "Brannock's camp. All."

Source: [12-the-storm-tree.md](../12-the-storm-tree.md)

## 13. The Quilled Hunter

A long, crooked line for the canyon, with a small cat drawn at its end: six legs and a back of quills like a hedgehog. Above it: "Wren's bow is here. Wren isn't." A row of small crosses runs along both sides of the canyon line.

Source: [13-the-quilled-hunter.md](../13-the-quilled-hunter.md)

## 14. The Shadow Court

A ring of five dead trees drawn in grey ink, each with a hooded cloak hanging from its branches. One cloak is filled in solid black. Beneath, in a shaky hand: "they watched me. they let me leave." The note has no date.

Source: [14-the-shadow-court.md](../14-the-shadow-court.md)

## 15. The Moody Brood

A circle divided into four wedges like a sundial, each coloured for a season: green, gold, rust and white. Two tiny curled dragons perch on its rim. Underneath: "charming!! bring the court." Below that, struck through twice: "never again."

Source: [15-the-moody-brood.md](../15-the-moody-brood.md)

## 16. The Trickster's Emissary

A raven inked in solid black with one eye left as a blank white circle. Beneath it, in a hurried hand: "the raven. it knew my name before I spoke. DO NOT RETURN." The ink is smeared where the pen dragged.

Source: [16-the-tricksters-emissary.md](../16-the-tricksters-emissary.md)

## 17. Spore-Dawn

A circle filled with tiny ink dots, an arrow pointing down into its centre, and the words "omen — of what?" in a careful astrologer's script. Below, in a heavier, angrier hand: "burn the astrologer."

Source: [17-spore-dawn.md](../17-spore-dawn.md)

## 18. The Linnorm's Pass

A dashed ink line marked "trade road west" running across the mountains, ending at a coiled serpent drawn in red ink with spines along its back. Beside it: "blocked — serpent. Sent 40 men. Sent 12 more. No."

Source: [18-the-linnorms-pass.md](../18-the-linnorms-pass.md)

## 19. Shadow on the Peaks

A black spire shaded in heavy ink. Above it, a wing shape drawn again and again, the lines growing darker and heavier with each pass. The note reads: "her old roost — empty? if so, mine."

Source: [19-shadow-on-the-peaks.md](../19-shadow-on-the-peaks.md)

## 20. The Burning Giant

A line of hexes coloured in red crayon, running from the mountains toward the lowlands. Beside it: "toward us — or toward them?" and an arrow pointing away from the King's city that has been scratched out and redrawn twice.

Source: [20-the-burning-giant.md](../20-the-burning-giant.md)

## 21. The Annihilator

A line of small burned-house glyphs running west out of the Skyfall Wastes, each one crossed through. At the end of the line: "no bodies — it takes them ALIVE. where?"

Source: [21-the-annihilator.md](../21-the-annihilator.md)
