# Characters

Portrait and token briefs for every creature and NPC, grouped by encounter.

## 01. The Shadowless Lodge

### The Ankou (Nyrissa's assassin)
- **Portrait:** A tall, faceless fey shape wrapped in folded black wings that hang like a cloak. Where a face should be there is only a smooth hollow of shadow. Long claws rest crossed in front of its body, and it holds a slip of fluttering darkness pinched between two fingers like a moth: a stolen shadow.
- **Token:** Seen from above, wings half-spread over the rafters, body crouched to drop, claws hooked over a beam.

### The Ankou's Shadow Double (first)
- **Portrait:** The ankou's silhouette rendered in smoke, edges fraying into wisps, its wings slightly mismatched as if poorly copied.
- **Token:** Wings swept back in a low glide, claws forward.

### The Ankou's Shadow Double (second)
- **Portrait:** The same shape, frozen mid-turn, its head tilted at an angle no living neck could manage.
- **Token:** Upright and still, wings folded, standing beside a pillar like a mourner.

### Felgo (dead Pitaxian scout)
- **Portrait:** A young man in Pitaxian green leathers, sunburned, a scout's bow over his shoulder, a sprig of heather pinned to his collar. His expression is that of someone hearing his own name whispered.
- **Token:** Face-down on a threshold, one hand stretched toward a door, no shadow beneath him.

Source: [01-the-shadowless-lodge.md](../01-the-shadowless-lodge.md)

## 02. The Perfect Song

### Maestra Ilsabet Rova (composer, leader)
- **Portrait:** A woman in her fifties with silver hair escaping a once-elegant braid, gaunt and hollow-cheeked, wearing a stained velvet Academy coat with gold piping. Ink stains her fingers to the knuckle. Her eyes are bright, wet and fixed on something far away; her lips shape a tune.
- **Token:** Cross-legged in grass, bent over a sheaf of pages, quill raised mid-stroke.

### Pell (youngest composer)
- **Portrait:** A boy of about seventeen, skeletal, collarbones sharp under a too-large shirt, a cracked lute in his lap. He smiles beatifically, unaware he is dying.
- **Token:** Slumped on his side in the grass, still clutching the lute.

### Daro Vesk (composer, percussionist)
- **Portrait:** A broad man gone loose and sallow, beard grown wild, tapping rhythms on his knees with bloodied fingertips. A drum lies split beside him.
- **Token:** Sitting upright, hands raised mid-beat.

### Olenna Fair (composer, copyist)
- **Portrait:** A neat, bespectacled woman whose court dress has been carefully mended, writing music in tiny precise notation across blank staves that show nothing at all.
- **Token:** Kneeling, writing on a page held flat against a stone.

### Cauthooj (first, the lead singer)
- **Portrait:** A tall flightless bird with a long neck and a heavy hooked beak, iridescent blue-black plumage, its throat puffed out and trembling with song. Its eyes are bright gold and unblinking.
- **Token:** Head thrown back, throat swollen in song.

### Cauthooj (second)
- **Portrait:** Leaner and greyer, with a torn crest and a scar across its beak, crouched low in the grass with its head swaying side to side.
- **Token:** Stalking low through grass, neck stretched forward.

### Cauthooj (third)
- **Portrait:** The youngest, smaller, with patchy juvenile feathers and goat's blood on its beak, standing on one leg in a pose of strange, alert grace.
- **Token:** Mid-hop, one leg tucked, wings flared for balance.

Source: [02-the-perfect-song.md](../02-the-perfect-song.md)

## 03. Nine Mouths, One Belly

### The Jotund Troll
- **Portrait:** A towering, hunched troll with mossy green-grey hide and nine heads on long, sinewy necks sprouting from its shoulders. The heads all argue at once. One head wears a neatly oiled moustache and a courtier's gold earring, mimicking a gentleman. Three hang drooling and hungry; three snarl with scarred brows; two are grey, wrinkled and half-asleep.
- **Token:** Seen from above, nine heads fanned out in different directions like a crown of serpents, claws planted on bones.

### The Old Heads (troll heads)

### Ser Halward Toll (dead envoy)

### Mulch (adolescent athach)

### Haddo (living statue, farmer)

### Ysolde (living statue, pedlar)

### Tobin (living statue, boy)
