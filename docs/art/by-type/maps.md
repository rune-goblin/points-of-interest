# Maps

Tactical maps for every site, plus any interior maps.

## 01. The Shadowless Lodge

### Tactical map

A clearing ringed by bare birch trees, every trunk casting a long shadow across the grass. In the centre stands a dark-timbered hunting lodge with a sagging antler-crowned gable and a collapsed patch of roof. A body lies across the front threshold with no shadow beneath it. Inside: a great hall with a gallery running around three sides, heavy rafters, a cold hearth with an earthenware jar on the mantle, and a wall of mounted elk and boar heads whose eyes all face the door. Off the hall, a bunkroom with five bedrolls, each holding a still figure. Scratches score the highest rafters.

Source: [01-the-shadowless-lodge.md](../01-the-shadowless-lodge.md)

## 02. The Perfect Song

### Tactical map

A high meadow at sunset, waist-high golden grass rolling toward a line of dark woods. At the centre, a trampled circle where four figures sit among drifts of paper weighted by stones, inkpots and a violin case. A ring of flattened grass about sixty feet out marks where the birds pace. At its edge, goat skulls and scattered bones, and one human hand still in a velvet cuff. A herders' track runs down one side toward the tourney fields below; a lone shepherd's hut stands at the woodline.

Source: [02-the-perfect-song.md](../02-the-perfect-song.md)

## 03. Nine Mouths, One Belly

### Tactical map

A grey-green peat moor dotted with cairns and dark bog pools. A long barrow mound rises in the centre, its entrance framed by standing stones carved with spirals. Beside the entrance, a wooden stake bearing a severed head, with a leather satchel hung below it. Inside the barrow: a long earthen chamber, floor carpeted with bones and dented armour, an alcove at the far end holding a cairn of huge, ancient bones draped in faded hides. Outside, patches of soft bog mark the approaches.

Source: [03-nine-mouths-one-belly.md](../03-nine-mouths-one-belly.md)

## 04. The Gardeners

### Tactical map

The roofless shell of a stone manor at one end, its upper floor collapsed into rubble. In front of it, a formal garden of overgrown topiary hedges forming a maze of corridors, with stone plinths at the junctions. Five plinths hold living people propped upright and bound with vines. A stinking compost pit of mulch and bones sits in a corner of the garden, and a broken fountain stands at the maze's heart. Shears, rakes and a huge wheelbarrow lie by the manor steps.

Source: [04-the-gardeners.md](../04-the-gardeners.md)

## 05. The Ossuary Wall

### Tactical map

A hilltop ruin on a windswept ridge. An outer ring of tumbled grey stone surrounds an inner wall made entirely of skulls, packed tight and weathered smooth, with three breaches in it. Near the top of the wall, four fresh white skulls, one still wearing wire spectacles. The inner court is open ground strewn with bone fragments, with a fallen standing stone at its centre hiding a sealed burial chamber beneath. Outside the wall, an abandoned scholars' camp: tents, a collapsed easel, sketchbooks blowing open in the wind, a surveyor's chain stretched across the grass.

Source: [05-the-ossuary-wall.md](../05-the-ossuary-wall.md)

## 06. The Larder

### Tactical map

A wooded hollow closed on the north by a granite cliff with a narrow switchback path climbing to a rock pile on top. In the centre, a large fire pit ringed with stones, a spit across it. Around the fire stand timber smoking racks hung with split carcasses. A sod-roofed smokehouse sits to the east, smoke leaking from its door and roof. Against the cliff stand three log cages, one holding a man in a torn clerk's coat, one a wiry scavenger, one a Kellid rider. Scattered barrels of salt, a chopping block with a cleaver buried in it, piled bones, and a broken prisoner wagon with a Pitaxian crest at the southern edge where the trail enters the hollow.

Source: [06-the-larder.md](../06-the-larder.md)

## 07. The Wyvern Queen's Hunting Ground

### Tactical map

A collapsed ridge seen from above: a slope of grey shale running down from south to north, crossed by snapped and fallen pines. Near the centre stands one tall dead pine with a mammoth carcass wedged in its crown. The north edge breaks off into a deep gorge, shown as a sharp drop with mist below. Bones of horses and men lie scattered in the shale, and a round shield with a crest of a laurel and mask lies half-buried near the cliff edge. A game trail enters from the south through standing pines.

Source: [07-the-wyvern-queens-hunting-ground.md](../07-the-wyvern-queens-hunting-ground.md)

## 08. The Lightwardens

### Tactical map

A grassy hillside with a huge wedge of silvery hull buried at a steep angle, its seams glowing with pale light and a round sealed hatch on its exposed flank. In front of the hull, a wide ring of glassy, scorched earth. Three tall metal pylons with lens heads stand in an arc inside the ring. Within the ring lie skeletons in Pitaxian livery, a dropped crowbar, a broken lantern and a pack spilling tools. At the edge of the ring, a large brass-and-glass device of nested rings and lenses stands motionless. Long grass and scattered boulders surround the scorched area.

Source: [08-the-lightwardens.md](../08-the-lightwardens.md)

## 09. The Iron Juggernaut

### Tactical map

Flat grassland crossed by a deep trench worn smooth by ten thousand years of tracks, its walls crumbling. At the northern bend, a rusted gantry of girders straddles the trench, scorched where something docks. At the southern bend, the trench ends in a glassed crater with a few stumps of ancient foundations. Beside the trench, an abandoned salvage camp: a toppled wagon, pry bars and chains, a collapsed tent, and a cracked plate of dark grey metal propped against the wagon. A few lone boulders in the grass.

### Interior map (cargo hold)

A long, low metal hold lit through cracks in the hull. The floor is deep in bones and rags. Scratched tally marks cover every wall, old ones worn faint, the newest sharp and bright. A dorsal hatch overhead. Two living captives huddle at the far end beside an empty water skin.

Source: [09-the-iron-juggernaut.md](../09-the-iron-juggernaut.md)

## 10. The Scout's Wager

### Tactical map

A shallow crater with sloping sides. At its bottom lies a blackened, split-open metal escape pod. Green threads run from the pod's ribs to stakes around the crater floor, strung with antlers, skulls and a torn Pitaxian cloak. Patches of fused glass shine across the crater floor. Low fog pools in the deepest parts. A skeleton in leather armour lies inside the pod beside a satchel. Grassland and scattered rocks surround the rim.

Source: [10-the-scouts-wager.md](../10-the-scouts-wager.md)

## 11. The Unmaker

### Tactical map

A broad crater with a floor of fused green glass, cracked in spiderweb lines that run toward the centre. At the heart, a giant construct stands buried to the waist, its chest plates daubed with chalk sigils. Timber scaffolding climbs its front to the collar. Copper cables run from its shoulders to a ring of six iron capacitor drums spaced evenly around the crater floor. A haze of glittering dust hangs around the giant. At the crater's rim, a covered wagon stands beside a campfire, a workbench and crates of tools. A rough track winds down the slope from the wagon to the scaffold.

Source: [11-the-unmaker.md](../11-the-unmaker.md)

## 12. The Storm Tree

### Tactical map

A forest clearing on a slope. The upper half is rows of charred stumps where trees were felled in neat lines. In the middle sits a half-burned logging camp: collapsed canvas tents, a rectangular sawpit, a cart heaped with split logs parked on the slope, and a foreman's tent with a half-buried iron strongbox beside it. At the centre of the camp stands an enormous blackened tree with glowing cracks in its bark and roots splayed across the ground. Scorch marks radiate through the soil around it. The living forest wall of Thousand Voices rises along the bottom edge.

Source: [12-the-storm-tree.md](../12-the-storm-tree.md)

## 13. The Quilled Hunter

### Tactical map

The den at the canyon's end. Steep rock walls on both sides close overhead under a forest canopy, letting in green light. Trophies hang from the walls on vine nets: a manticore's tail, a cracked iron crown, a small dragon skull. Two dense thorn thickets fill the middle of the canyon floor. A fallen log bridges a narrow crevasse near the entrance. At the far end, a sandy hollow lies under a huge fallen tree, scattered with bones, a ranger's pack and an unstrung longbow.

Source: [13-the-quilled-hunter.md](../13-the-quilled-hunter.md)

## 14. The Shadow Court

### Tactical map

A round clearing of grey moss in a dead forest. Five huge dead trees form a ring, their branches hung with dozens of empty grey cloaks. A stone table stands at the centre with a single untouched place setting: plate, goblet and knife. At the head of the table stands a carved chair draped in black silk. A badge of Pitax is pinned to one of the hanging cloaks. Dim light filters through the dead branches.

Source: [14-the-shadow-court.md](../14-the-shadow-court.md)

## 15. The Moody Brood

### Tactical map

A round grove divided into four quarters by season. Spring has blossoming trees and muddy ground. Summer has lush grass. Autumn has falling red leaves. Winter has snow-covered ground and frosted branches. A large mossy stone sits at the centre where the quarters meet. Beside it lies a ruined banquet table with overturned goblets, a jester's cap and a broken lute. Silver plates lie scattered in the grass.

Source: [15-the-moody-brood.md](../15-the-moody-brood.md)

## 16. The Lantern King's Emissary

### Tactical map

A round clearing in old-growth forest, floored with moss and fallen leaves. Seven standing stones, moss-covered and lichen-streaked, ring the clearing. An eighth stone lies toppled outside the ring, its face carved with a lantern-bearing figure and kneeling worshippers. Huge tree trunks crowd the edge of the clearing, their roots buckling the ground. Shafts of light fall through gaps in the canopy onto the centre, where a large patch of crushed moss marks where the zomok lands.

Source: [16-the-lantern-kings-emissary.md](../16-the-lantern-kings-emissary.md)

## 17. Spore-Dawn

### Tactical map

A sinkhole in a grey, dying forest. The trees around the rim have peeling bark and pale spore-dust drifting from their trunks; twenty of them bear visible fungal growths. The sinkhole drops to a floor carpeted in white mould and fungal stalks. On one edge of the rim, a collapsed wooden hunting blind lies half-buried in mould, a body slumped inside. Above the sinkhole hangs the vast bulk of the mu spore, its tentacles trailing down into the trees below.

Source: [17-spore-dawn.md](../17-spore-dawn.md)

## 18. The Linnorm's Pass

### Tactical map

A high mountain pass of grey scree between two cliff faces. A broad avalanche chute runs down one side, choked with boulders. Near the head of the pass, a crack in the mountainside glows with molten rock, and steam rises from it. Drifts of bones in rusted green-and-gold Pitaxian armour lie heaped against the rocks along the trail. The pass narrows to two tight points where the cliffs close in.

Source: [18-the-linnorms-pass.md](../18-the-linnorms-pass.md)

## 19. Shadow on the Peaks

### Tactical map

A black-rock spire rising out of a misty fen of dark, still water. The fen is dotted with dead trees and pools stained yellow-green. A narrow, broken path winds up the spire to a ledge near its top, where an open cave mouth is strewn with bones and pitted metal. Old wooden survey stakes with faded green ribbons ring the cave entrance.

Source: [19-shadow-on-the-peaks.md](../19-shadow-on-the-peaks.md)

## 20. The Burning Giant

### Tactical map

A scorched valley with a small herding village, Kettle Hollow, at its centre: twelve thatched buildings, several on fire, with a grain cellar and a stone well-house. A cold stream runs along the south edge of the valley. Shards of black glass litter the ground in a trail leading from the north ridge to the village. Smoke and cinders drift across the scene.

Source: [20-the-burning-giant.md](../20-the-burning-giant.md)

## 21. The Annihilator

### Tactical map

A hillside farm on the Numerian border. A stone farmhouse with a root-cellar door at its back. A wooden barn burning. A goat pasture ringed by a dry-stone wall, goats scattering. A well, a woodpile and an overturned cart in the yard. A track runs east over the hill toward Numeria, with smoke rising from other burned steadings on the horizon.

Source: [21-the-annihilator.md](../21-the-annihilator.md)
