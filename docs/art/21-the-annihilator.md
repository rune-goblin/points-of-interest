# 21. The Annihilator — Art

## Map note
A line of small burned-house glyphs running west out of Numeria, each one crossed through. At the end of the line: "no bodies — it takes them ALIVE. where?"

## Tactical map
A hillside farm on the Numerian border. A stone farmhouse with a root-cellar door at its back. A wooden barn burning. A goat pasture ringed by a dry-stone wall, goats scattering. A well, a woodpile and an overturned cart in the yard. A track runs east over the hill toward Numeria, with smoke rising from other burned steadings on the horizon.

## Characters

### Annihilator robot
- **Portrait:** A massive four-legged war machine of dark grey adamantine plating scarred by ages of use, with clawed legs and a long segmented tail curled over its back, ending in a plasma lance emitter. Twin chain gun barrels on its shoulders. A sensor cluster glows red on its head plate. Four armoured hatches line its flank; one bulges as if struck from inside.
- **Token:** Seen from above, four splayed clawed legs, tail curled forward over the body, gun barrels pointed ahead.

### Wenna Corl (goatherd in stasis)
- **Portrait:** A sturdy young human woman in a herder's smock and shawl, frozen mid-shout behind a frosted panel, a shepherd's crook in her hand.
- **Token:** Standing, crook raised, looking dazed.

### Aldo Corl (farmer)
- **Portrait:** A weathered middle-aged human man with a soot-streaked face and a pitchfork, standing between his family and the cellar stairs.
- **Token:** Braced with the pitchfork forward.

### Hesk Corl (grandmother)
- **Portrait:** A small, fierce old woman in a headscarf, clutching a lantern and a rolling pin, eyes defiant.
- **Token:** Hunched, lantern held high.

### Pell Corl (son)
- **Portrait:** A boy of about eight with ash in his hair, holding a wooden toy sword in front of his sister.
- **Token:** Standing guard, toy sword raised.

### Mira Corl (daughter)
- **Portrait:** A girl of about five clutching a kid goat to her chest, wide-eyed and smudged with soot.
- **Token:** Crouched, hugging the goat.
