# 01. The Shadowless Lodge — Art

## Map note
A quick ink sketch of a lodge with an antlered gable. Beside it, where a shadow should fall, an empty circle traced twice. Scrawled beneath: "scouts lost — 3rd time. No shadows. Who?"

## Tactical map
A clearing ringed by bare birch trees, every trunk casting a long shadow across the grass. In the centre stands a dark-timbered hunting lodge with a sagging antler-crowned gable and a collapsed patch of roof. A body lies across the front threshold with no shadow beneath it. Inside: a great hall with a gallery running around three sides, heavy rafters, a cold hearth with an earthenware jar on the mantle, and a wall of mounted elk and boar heads whose eyes all face the door. Off the hall, a bunkroom with five bedrolls, each holding a still figure. Scratches score the highest rafters.

## Characters

### The Ankou (the Fey Queen's assassin)
- **Portrait:** A tall, faceless fey shape wrapped in folded black wings that hang like a cloak. Where a face should be there is only a smooth hollow of shadow. Long claws rest crossed in front of its body, and it holds a slip of fluttering darkness pinched between two fingers like a moth: a stolen shadow.
- **Token:** Seen from above, wings half-spread over the rafters, body crouched to drop, claws hooked over a beam.

### The Ankou's Shadow Double (first)
- **Portrait:** The ankou's silhouette rendered in smoke, edges fraying into wisps, its wings slightly mismatched as if poorly copied.
- **Token:** Wings swept back in a low glide, claws forward.

### The Ankou's Shadow Double (second)
- **Portrait:** The same shape, frozen mid-turn, its head tilted at an angle no living neck could manage.
- **Token:** Upright and still, wings folded, standing beside a pillar like a mourner.

### Felgo (dead royal scout)
- **Portrait:** A young man in royal green leathers, sunburned, a scout's bow over his shoulder, a sprig of heather pinned to his collar. His expression is that of someone hearing his own name whispered.
- **Token:** Face-down on a threshold, one hand stretched toward a door, no shadow beneath him.
