# 14. The Shadow Court — Art

## Map note
A ring of five dead trees drawn in grey ink, each with a hooded cloak hanging from its branches. One cloak is filled in solid black. Beneath, in a shaky hand: "they watched me. they let me leave." The note has no date.

## Tactical map
A round clearing of grey moss in a dead forest. Five huge dead trees form a ring, their branches hung with dozens of empty grey cloaks. A stone table stands at the centre with a single untouched place setting: plate, goblet and knife. At the head of the table stands a carved chair draped in black silk. A badge of Pitax is pinned to one of the hanging cloaks. Dim light filters through the dead branches.

## Characters

### The Speaker (ankou assassin)
- **Portrait:** A tall, gaunt fey wrapped in wings like a shroud of smoke. Its face is smooth and eyeless, with a thin mouth that never opens. Black claws rest on its chest. A silver circlet sits on its brow, marking its rank.
- **Token:** Standing at the head of the table beside the empty chair, wings half-spread, head tilted.

### First ankou
- **Portrait:** A slender, eyeless fey with grey wings folded tight, face turned slightly away.
- **Token:** Perched on a high branch, crouched, wings folded.

### Second ankou
- **Portrait:** A broad-shouldered ankou with tattered wing edges and a scar across its featureless face.
- **Token:** Kneeling on the moss, claws resting on the ground.

### Third ankou
- **Portrait:** A small, sharp-featured ankou with wings that trail like smoke into the air.
- **Token:** Hanging upside down from a branch like a bat.

### Fourth ankou
- **Portrait:** A tall ankou with long, blade-like claws held crossed in front of it.
- **Token:** Standing behind a tree, half-hidden, wings spread wide.

### Spared ankou (optional, from the Shadowless Lodge)
- **Portrait:** An ankou with a missing claw and a pale stripe across one wing where the PCs' attack struck it.
- **Token:** Standing apart from the others, head bowed toward the party.
